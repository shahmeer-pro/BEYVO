# BEYVO

> Modern commerce infrastructure for building, selling, and scaling online.

BEYVO is a modern, scalable commerce platform designed to give businesses everything they need to launch, manage, and grow their online commerce operations from a single platform.

## Overview

BEYVO aims to provide a powerful alternative to traditional ecommerce platforms by combining a flexible storefront system with the infrastructure businesses need to manage products, customers, orders, inventory, payments, analytics, and more.

The platform is designed with scalability, performance, security, and extensibility in mind.

## Core Features

- 🛍️ Storefront Management
- 📦 Product & Inventory Management
- 🛒 Cart & Checkout
- 💳 Payment Integration
- 📋 Order Management
- 👥 Customer Management
- 📊 Analytics & Reporting
- 🎟️ Discounts & Promotions
- 🚚 Shipping Management
- 🔐 Authentication & Authorization
- ⚡ High-performance APIs
- 🔌 Extensible Integrations
- 🤖 Automation & AI-ready Architecture
